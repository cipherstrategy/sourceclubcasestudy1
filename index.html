Match Confidence Framework

95-100% · Auto Approve

Clean Match

Exact SKU/catalog identifier, equivalent unit, and no risk flags.

80-94% · Exception Review

Human Review

Likely equivalent but missing SKU, pack-size mismatch, house brand, or substitution judgment.

<80% · Manual Research

Do Not Auto-Match

Not enough evidence to safely include in the report without human research.

Efficiency rule: The founder only reviews exceptions. Clean SKU/unit matches are auto-approved, ambiguous items are routed to review, and risky low-confidence matches are blocked from the final savings claim.

Why This Architecture Works

AI handles repetitive analysis work, humans review exceptions, and the system learns from each approved decision.

AI Layer

  • Normalize supplier exports
  • Enrich missing SKUs
  • Match products across catalogs
  • Score confidence
  • Explain recommendations

Human Layer

  • Review exception items
  • Approve substitutions
  • Validate sensitive products
  • Confirm high-impact opportunities

Learning Layer

  • Store approved matches
  • Capture reviewer decisions
  • Improve future matching
  • Build Match Memory
Core thesis: The goal is not to remove the founder's judgment. The goal is to eliminate repetitive spreadsheet work, auto-approve clean matches, route unclear line items to a fast human review queue, and convert founder knowledge into a repeatable process.
